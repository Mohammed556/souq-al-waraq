الصيادThe Hunter

Every Monday it looks at 9 popular intraday strategies traders share online (VWAP pullback, EMA 9/21 cross, RSI(2), Bollinger bounce, MACD, volume breakout, gap-and-go, 15- and 30-minute opening breakouts) and uses the one with the best record over the last 40 sessions.
